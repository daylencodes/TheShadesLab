FAQ

Common questions

Straight answers before you reach out—no obligation to enquire.

How long does a typical project take?

Residential rooms often complete within 2–4 weeks from final measurement, depending on fabric availability. Commercial and hospitality timelines vary by scale—we provide a clear schedule at specification stage.

Do you offer on-site measurement?

Yes. We measure on-site to ensure a precise, made-to-fit result. Showroom visits at 63@Ubi are also welcome if you prefer to review fabrics in person first.

Which systems work best in Singapore's heat and humidity?

Solar-screen rollers, honeycomb blinds, and reflective fabrics are popular for reducing heat gain. We recommend options based on your window orientation, AC usage, and design goals.

Are your products child-safe?

We supply cord-free and compliant child-safe systems suitable for family homes. Ask our team about the latest Singapore guidelines during your consultation.

Can blinds be motorised and connected to smart home?

Yes. We integrate motorised shading with leading automation partners for app and voice control—ideal for hard-to-reach windows and unified smart homes.

Is there a warranty on installation?

We stand behind our workmanship and will clarify warranty terms for products and installation during your quote. Maintenance guidance is provided at handover.
